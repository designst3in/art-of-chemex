# 07 · Brew Method

## Goal

A repeatable Chemex brew for the workshop — clear enough for beginners, flexible for coffee nerds.

## Step sequence

1. Rinse filter with hot water; discard rinse water (preheats vessel).
2. Add grounds; level the bed.
3. Bloom: pour ~2× coffee weight in water; wait ~30–45 s.
4. Continue pouring in concentric circles / pulses to target yield.
5. Allow drain; remove filter; swirl; serve.

## Sensory teaching notes

- Expect a **clean** cup relative to metal-filter methods (paper retention of oils/fines — discuss, don’t dogmatize).
- Defects to demo: channeling (uneven bed), grind too fine (stall), grind too coarse (under-extraction).
