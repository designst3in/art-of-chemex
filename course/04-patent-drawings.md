# 04 · Patent Drawings

## Learning goal

Teach participants how to **read** patent drawings (views, reference numerals, claims vs. figures) using Chemex-related filings as a case study — without inventing numbers.

## How to use this module

1. Locate the relevant Chemex / Schlumbohm patents on **USPTO** or **Google Patents**.
2. Save PDFs or figure exports under `assets/patents/` (respect site terms).
3. Record number, title, date, and URL in `assets/SOURCES.md`.
4. Walk the room through: title page → figures → short claim language (plain English).
