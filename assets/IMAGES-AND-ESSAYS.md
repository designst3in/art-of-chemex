## D. Design studies, papers, essays

Each entry: citation · URL · 3-sentence annotation.

### Museum / curator essays

1. **Gordon, John Stuart.** “Curator’s Eye: Chemex Coffeemaker by Peter Schlumbohm.” *Modern Magazine*, 15 Jan 2019.  
   http://modernmag.com/curators-eye-chemex-coffeemaker-by-peter-schlumbohm/  
   Yale University Art Gallery curator frames the Chemex as socially conscious wartime design using non-priority glass, wood, and leather. Emphasizes Erlenmeyer/borosilicate lineage and Corning Pyrex manufacture paired with Chemex Corp. assembly. Argues the kitchen-as-laboratory aesthetic carried into postwar “atomic age” domestic ideals.

2. **Teichman, Susan.** “The Perfect Cup, Coffee Brewed to Perfection.” Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum Object of the Day, 6 Apr 2018.  
   https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2018/04/06/the-perfect-cup-coffee-brewed-to-perfection/  
   Museum essay on the “Chemist’s Kitchen,” Bauhaus form-follows-function, and non-metal wartime patriotism. Notes IIT’s “100 best-designed products of modern times” recognition. Strong short teaching text for workshop slide decks.

3. **Museum of Modern Art.** Online collection wall text for *Chemex Coffee Maker* (1941).  
   https://www.moma.org/collection/works/1847  
   Quotes Schlumbohm’s functional credo and situates the piece in Bauhaus-inspired immigrant design culture. Positions Chemex among ~300 patents and as his most successful domestic design. Essential primary museum interpretation (image rights separate).

4. **Museum of Modern Art.** *Useful Objects in Wartime* bulletin (1942–43).  
   https://assets.moma.org/documents/moma_catalogue_2733_300164646.pdf  
   Primary exhibition document with Chemex on the cover and listed as item 56. Articulates MoMA criteria of function, materials, technique, and synthesis while rejecting decorative streamlining. Downloaded for workshop reading packet.

### Long-form profiles & design journalism

5. **Oatman-Stanford, Hunter.** “Mr. Chemex: The Eccentric Inventor Who Reimagined the Perfect Cup of Coffee.” *Collectors Weekly*.  
   https://www.collectorsweekly.com/articles/mr-chemex/  
   Best single popular biography: Beautilities, MoMA holdings count, LIFE 1949 formula (20% problem / 40% patent / 30% design / 10% merchandising), and marketing eccentricities. Draws on interviews and company lore; rich for storytelling though secondary. Companion Medium mirror: https://medium.com/hunter-oatman-stanford/mr-chemex-the-eccentric-inventor-who-reimagined-the-perfect-cup-of-coffee-cad87e2e49ff

6. **Rao, Tejal.** “Dr. Chemex.” *Gourmet*, 10 Jun 2008.  
   http://www.gourmet.com.s3-website-us-east-1.amazonaws.com/food/2008/06/schlumbohm.html  
   Literary profile of Schlumbohm as bon vivant inventor; covers Tempot failure, Time magazine coverage, and Chemex’s Bauhaus aura. Useful counterweight to pure design-history framing. Archived Gourmet URL still resolves as of research date.

7. **Dupuy, Jessica.** “Iconic Design: The Chemex Brewer.” *Imbibe Magazine*, 23 Feb 2016.  
   https://imbibemagazine.com/iconic-design-the-chemex-brewer/  
   Beverage-industry design feature cited by Wikipedia Chemex article. Bridges third-wave coffee culture and mid-century modern object history. Good for audience already in specialty coffee.

8. **The New Yorker.** “Schlumbohm Again.” 12 Jun 1954.  
   https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/1954/06/12/schlumbohm-again  
   Contemporary visit with Schlumbohm discussing the Chemobile and lifestyle; vivid primary-color anecdote source. Copyrighted full text; cite/quote sparingly under fair use.

### Ralph Caplan (eulogy trail)

9. **Caplan, Ralph.** Frequently cited 1962 eulogy characterizing Schlumbohm inventions as “a synthesis of logic and madness,” and Chemex as “one of the few modern designs for which one can feel affection as well as admiration.”  
   Secondary attestations: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Schlumbohm ; https://boisecoffee.org/podcast/s2-episode-9-chemex-synthesis-logic-madness/  
   **Original publication venue not independently confirmed online this session** (Caplan was editor of *Industrial Design* until ~1963; a Dec 1962 / early 1963 *I.D.* issue is the likeliest home, but no digitized issue with the eulogy was fetched). Treat the famous phrases as Caplan’s, cite via Wikipedia/Hagley until original page scan is obtained.

10. **Caplan, Ralph.** Discussion of Schlumbohm / Chemex / “Phlebette” in Core77 “By Design: A Conversation.”  
    https://www.core77.com/posts/99942/By-Design-A-Conversation  
    Caplan himself recalls Schlumbohm as beloved “crazy inventor” character from his book *By Design*. Confirms Caplan’s long engagement with Chemex as design-criticism case study. Use alongside search for the 1962 magazine original.

### Archival / scholarly / oral history

11. **Barwick, Clark.** “Tasteful Design: Peter Schlumbohm & the Chemex Coffeemaker.” Hagley History Hangout (podcast/video).  
    https://www.hagley.org/research/history-hangout-16  
    Cultural historian (Indiana University) working from Hagley Schlumbohm scrapbooks (Acc. 2193). Best scholarly *oral* entry into the papers; YouTube: https://youtu.be/fDes5oQSsAk  
    Frame as research-in-progress / public humanities rather than peer-reviewed journal article.

12. **SNAC / Hagley finding aid abstract** for Schlumbohm papers.  
    https://snaccooperative.org/ark:/99166/w6dz0zpr · https://findingaids.hagley.org/repositories/3/resources/983  
    Archival authority record summarizing inventor-as-scientist self-image, Bauhaus affinities, and elite vs. mass-market reception. Points researchers to correspondence (Addams, Truman, LBJ) and Moscow kitchen-debate Chemex photos.

13. **Lemelson-MIT.** “Peter Schlumbohm” inventor profile.  
    https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/peter-schlumbohm  
    Concise patent-and-biography overview suitable for handouts. Notes IIT 1956 recognition and museum collections.

### Victor Papanek / Green Imperative

14. **Papanek, Victor J.** *The Green Imperative: Natural Design for the Real World.* Thames & Hudson, 1995. ISBN 0-500-27846-6.  
    Cited on Wikipedia *Chemex Coffeemaker* (ref. for design-section material on the wooden collar / appearance).  
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemex_Coffeemaker · book info https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/the-green-imperative
