# 01 · History & Impact

## Snapshot

- **Object:** Chemex pour-over coffee maker
- **Approx. introduction:** ~**1941**
- **Inventor:** Peter Schlumbohm
- **Signature form:** borosilicate glass hourglass + wood collar (with tie)

## Teaching beats

1. Place the Chemex in the mid-century design / consumer culture timeline.
2. Distinguish *invention* (lab-glass thinking applied to coffee) from *brand* (how the object circulated).
3. Note lasting cultural footprint: design museums, home kitchens, specialty coffee revival.

## Scaffold notes

The Chemex emerged in the early 1940s as a single, elegant vessel for brewing and serving. Its clarity of form helped it travel from domestic use into design canons (including MoMA’s design collection — see Module 02).
