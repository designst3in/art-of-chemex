# Art of Chemex

A workshop exploring the **Chemex** coffee maker as design object, invention, and brew method.

## Overview

The Chemex is an hourglass-shaped pour-over coffee maker introduced around **1941** by German chemist and inventor **Peter Schlumbohm**. Its borosilicate glass vessel, wood collar, and leather (or similar) tie have become icons of mid-century industrial design — including a place in the **Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)** design collection.

This Docsify site is the course home for the *Art of Chemex* workshop: history, design reading, patent visuals, brew practice, and a run-of-show for facilitators.
