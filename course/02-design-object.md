# 02 · Design Object

## Form language

- **Vessel:** hourglass / double-cone silhouette in **borosilicate** glass
- **Collar:** wood band at the waist
- **Tie:** leather (or similar) fastening — functional + visual punctuation
- **Filter:** proprietary thick paper filter (shape follows the upper cone)

## Why designers care

The Chemex reads as industrial design reduced to essentials: transparent process, honest materials, and a silhouette that photographs well. It is often cited in collections of modern design, including the **Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)** design collection.
