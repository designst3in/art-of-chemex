## Writing prompts for participants

1. **50-word object label** — as if for a design museum.
2. **Material honesty paragraph** — glass, wood, paper as a system.
3. **Compare & contrast** — Chemex vs. another pour-over (e.g. ceramic dripper) on *design*, not just taste.
